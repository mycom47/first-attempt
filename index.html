Beam Deflection

A cantilever beam is fixed at one end and free at the other — think of a diving board, or a balcony. Push down on the free end, and it bends. Engineers calculate exactly how much with one classic formula:

δ = PL³ / (3EI)

Deflection depends on the load (P), the beam's length (L), and its stiffness (E times I — the material and cross-section combined).
